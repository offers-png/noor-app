# Canonical Quran fixture provenance

Only five surahs are bundled: 1, 107, 112, 113, 114 (29 ayahs). The fixture builder copied source strings directly; no model generated or changed Quran text.

Arabic: Tanzil Project, Uthmani v1.1, downloaded 2026-10-07 from the official download endpoint selected via https://tanzil.net/download/. The exact publisher notice is embedded in quran-seed.json and quran-seed-verbatim.txt and in quran-tanzil-license.txt. License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 with the publisher's explicit requirement to keep Quran text unchanged. Source: https://tanzil.net/ ; license: https://tanzil.net/docs/Text_License . All 114 chapter names, counts, and revelation types come from https://tanzil.net/res/text/metadata/quran-data.xml . Metadata is distributed by Tanzil alongside its Quran text.

English: Translation by Talal Itani, ClearQuran.com (Allah Edition). Download: https://clearquran.com/downloads/quran-in-english-clearquran-verse-by-verse-txt-edition-allah.zip . Each verse-by-verse text file is copied unchanged. Publisher license checked 2026-10-07: https://blog.clearquran.com/download . CC BY-ND 4.0, with additional express permission to convert formats, quote selected verses, and combine with content while preserving all wording and attribution. The current publisher page supersedes older noncommercial readme notices. License evidence copies are retained alongside fixtures; their HTML is evidence only, never run or presented to children. Checksums of both downloaded artifacts appear in quran-seed.json.

Arabic audio: Mishary Rashid Alafasy, Islamic Network / Al Quran Cloud edition ar.alafasy, original 128 kbps clips for all 29 seeded ayahs, downloaded 2026-10-07. Manifest endpoints: https://api.alquran.cloud/v1/surah/1/ar.alafasy (and 107, 112, 113, 114). Original clips are copied byte-for-byte to assets/quran; quran-audio-manifest.json retains each source URL, byte size, edition, reciter name and SHA-256. Publisher source docs: https://alquran.cloud/cdn . Publisher terms checked 2026-10-07: https://alquran.cloud/terms-and-conditions (last updated 2026-06-14), which expressly permit personal and educational downloads, and bundling into commercial products, while reciters retain copyright and may request removal. No audio is generated, revoiced, sped up, or edited. At runtime these included assets play offline without network access. The license page is retained in quran-audio-license.html.

No transliteration, word meanings, tafsir, or Arabic recitation are invented. The 29 bundled transliteration rows are sourced separately as described below. Additional layers can be supplied by licensed provider snapshots. When a published layer is unavailable, the reader reports it explicitly. Canonical seeded ayahs, translation, and recordings are source content, separate from original educational explanations and the editorial review workflow.

Published meaning and notes: the five included surahs additionally carry the unchanged English meanings from QuranEnc's Rowwad Translation Center edition `english_rwwad`, version 1.0.19, verified 2026-10-08. All 29 ayahs have published meaning; 12 have publisher-supplied notes. Complete verse strings, notes where present, publisher metadata, version, exact API response bytes, checksums, and permission evidence are retained. This layer is labeled **Published meaning and notes**, not app-reviewed tafsir or original teaching. It is independently permitted publisher content, not an ordinary Quran Foundation API cache. See [quran-meaning-SOURCES.md](./quran-meaning-SOURCES.md) for official endpoints and all publisher reuse/update conditions.

Quran Foundation: https://api-docs.quran.com/docs/tutorials/content-sync/getting-started/ ; client flow: https://api-docs.quran.com/docs/tutorials/content-sync/client-flow/ ; recovery: https://api-docs.quran.com/docs/tutorials/content-sync/full-copies-and-recovery/ . Verified 2026-10-07. Offline QF content is obtained ONLY through Content Sync. Bootstrap snapshots, all pages, and the final token commit together; tokens are scoped by canonical resources filter and environment. Sync is due at least every seven days when parent-enabled connectivity permits and promptly on restoration. The production canonical singleton is quran_core:1; prelive's limited dataset does not meet the full canonical contract. Bundled Tanzil/ClearQuran data are independent licensed fixtures, never a cache of ordinary QF responses.

Runtime ayah audio URLs preserve the source path in the snapshot. Relative playback paths are resolved against https://verses.quran.com/, matching Quran Foundation's official SDK adapter: https://github.com/quran/api-js/blob/main/packages/api/src/sdk/audio.ts . Optional word timing comes only from the selected ayah audio file's segments; chapter-file timestamps are never applied to a separate ayah recording. Tafsir coverage follows the official inclusive start_verse_id/end_verse_id range: https://api-docs.quran.foundation/docs/tutorials/content-sync/full-copies-and-recovery/ .

## English transliteration (educational test APK)

Publisher: Tanzil Project, edition `en.transliteration`, credited by its own footer as **English Transliteration**, last updated September 6, 2010. Source download: https://tanzil.net/trans/en.transliteration . The publisher does not identify an individual transliterator in this edition; the app retains the published credit rather than guessing a name. Verified/downloaded 2026-10-08.

Permission: the publisher's translations page, https://tanzil.net/trans/ , explicitly permits **noncommercial purposes only** and requires permission from the translator or publisher for other uses. These terms apply to the transliteration edition; the separate Arabic Quran CC BY 3.0 license does not cover it. This fixture is supplied for the educational test APK. A commercial release using this transliteration needs the required permission or a replacement with commercial redistribution rights. No permission beyond the published terms has been obtained.

Only the 29 complete source rows matching surahs 1, 107, 112, 113 and 114 are bundled. `quran-transliteration.json` contains the unchanged per-verse strings, original publisher edition notice and source/checksum metadata. `quran-transliteration-verbatim.txt` preserves those source rows and the publisher notice for independent comparison. Original mixed-case `<u>` and `<b>` tags are retained as formatting markup in the data; a text renderer may display the underlining/bold formatting without changing the wording. No transliteration is generated, corrected, modernized or expanded. The whole verse rows must not be split into independently aligned Quran word data: this source does not provide a verified Arabic word alignment.

`quran-transliteration-license.html` retains the exact Terms of Use section from the publisher page, marked as an excerpt. It is evidence only and is never executed or rendered to children; the publisher's catalog of translations is omitted.
