Full offline Arabic and transliteration are separate publisher editions installed by a parent. Neither is AI generated. The installer preserves the full original publisher text including its notice and verifies byte identity before saving atomically to SQLite. It rejects a changed or damaged edition until source verification is updated. All 114 chapter counts and 6,236 ordered ayah identities are checked.

English tafsir is a distinct layer. No bundled Rowwad translation or footnotes are labeled tafsir. The currently published QuranEnc English catalog <https://quranenc.com/api/v1/translations/list/en> does not list an English Mokhtasar resource (checked 2026-10-08). Mokhtasr's public English book availability is not redistribution permission: <https://mokhtasr.com/en/pages/terms-and-conditions> restricts copies to personal noncommercial use and grants no app license. This app therefore does not scrape or bundle that book.

Configured Quran Foundation service: publisher-listed English tafsir can be displayed and saved only through supported Content Sync snapshots, with actual language/author/resource metadata and actual passage coverage. Resource IDs are discovered in the configured environment; none is assumed to be an English tafsir. Ordinary verses/tafsir API results are not cached for offline use. Developer Terms <https://api-docs.quran.com/legal/developer-terms/> and Content Sync documentation <https://api-docs.quran.com/docs/tutorials/content-sync/getting-started/> require ongoing refresh when connectivity allows; unavailable snapshots do not authorize a substitute offline copy. At the time of implementation, no local Quran Foundation credentials were present, so live English tafsir availability and full passage coverage require the user's configured authorized backend. No app-generated religious explanation or qualified-review claim is added.
